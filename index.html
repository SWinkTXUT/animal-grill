How to play

Move your chef cat

Press the ← ↑ ↓ → arrow keys (or WASD). On a tablet or phone, just touch and drag anywhere on the floor and your cat walks toward your finger.

Cook the food

When an animal orders, the order lines up at the grill (burgers), fryer (fries) or soda machine (soda). A machine with a bouncing ! is waiting — walk your cat up to it and it starts cooking all by itself.

Serve the food

Cooked food appears on the counter. Walk past to scoop it up (you can carry 3), then walk up to a hungry animal and they'll take it. Walk to the drive-thru window to hand food to a car.

Get some help

A cook runs one machine so you don't have to — there's a cook for the grill, the fryer, the soda machine and the milkshake machine. A waiter carries food out to the tables, and drive-thru staff only serve cars so your waiters can stay on the tables. Tap any of your crew in the Staff screen to see their profile, rename them, or move them to a different job.

Ingredients 🥫

Food isn't free! Every burger, fry, soda and shake uses ingredients you bought in the Pantry. What you charge minus what the ingredients cost is your real profit — the Prices screen shows it for every item. If a shelf runs empty that food comes off the menu until you restock, so keep an eye on it. Better suppliers in the Shop make ingredients cheaper forever.

Branding deals 🤝

Other animal businesses will pay to put their name in your restaurant. You pay a one-off fee and the deal runs 30 days. A partner brings in more of their animal and pushes one menu item — but you only get the extra customers if the deal suits you: you need the machines to make what they push, a price in their band, and food the animals they bring actually like. Check the fit meter before you sign, or you'll just lose the fee.

Who likes what 🐾

Every animal has a favourite and something they rarely order. Dogs love burgers but skip soda, bunnies love fries but skip burgers, hamsters love soda but skip milkshakes, and cats love milkshakes but skip fries.

Milkshakes 🥤

Buy a Milkshake Machine in the Shop to add Milkshakes to your menu. They take the longest to make but sell for the most.

Employee of the Month 🏆

Every 30 days you get to crown one member of your crew Employee of the Month. They wear a gold crown and work noticeably faster for the next 30 days. Their trophy count is kept forever on their profile.

Settings ⚙️

Tap ⚙️ to choose the hours your restaurant is open. Staying open longer brings in more animals and more Cat Dollars, but each day takes longer to play. Music and Reset Game live there too.

Grow the place

More tables = more animals at once. More machines = more food cooking at once. A drive-thru brings in cars all day long — extra money that doesn't need a table.

Reset everything? 🗑

This erases your restaurant name, your Cat Dollars, all your equipment and your whole crew. You'll start over on Day 1. This can't be undone!
